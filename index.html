Teguh Iklas M

Chairman OF DL Group

Teguh Iklas M. is the Chairman of DL Group, a holding company that oversees various industrial sectors.

Teguh Iklas M. serves as the Chairman of DL Group, a holding company that oversees a diverse range of industries. Under his leadership, DL Group manages five business entities operating in the sectors of franchise, culinary (F&B), energy, transportation, and property. These five entities are CV Anugerah Bintang Bersama, Dapoerluna Catering & Resto, PT Energi Tsost Bersama (PT ETB), PT Trans Explore Maju Bersama (PT TEMS), and PT Arsy Muda Propertindo (PT AMP). Each of these companies focuses on delivering high-quality services, ranging from franchise systems, food and catering services, the oil and gas sector, transportation services, to property management. With a strong vision and strategic leadership, Teguh Iklas M. continues to drive DL Group forward as a dynamic business group that makes meaningful contributions to society.
